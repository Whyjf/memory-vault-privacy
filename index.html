Memory Vault Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026 · Developer whyjf

Memory Vault is a private, offline journal for memories and dreams, made by whyjf. Everything you write stays on your phone. The app has no internet access, and I never see your entries.

What Memory Vault stores

Memory Vault stores your memories, threads and dreams, an optional photo for each memory, and your settings. All of it lives in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps can't read. Each save also keeps one backup copy in that same private storage, so a crash can't wipe your vault.

No internet, no account, no ads

Memory Vault does not have the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere on its own. There is no account or sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party crash reporting. I have no server and no way to see what you write.

Permissions

Memory Vault asks for one Android permission, Vibrate, for a small buzz when you touch things. It also declares a permission that an Android library adds automatically, which only Memory Vault itself can use.

It does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files, and it has no access to your photo library. When you add a photo to a memory, you choose that photo yourself, and Memory Vault keeps its own copy of only that photo in its private storage.

Passphrase lock

You can turn on an optional passphrase lock. When it's on, your vault, its backup copy and your photos are encrypted on the phone with a key made from your passphrase.

Backup files you export

You can export a backup file whenever you choose. It is sealed with a password unless you choose a readable copy, and it goes only where you save it. Once it's saved outside the app, where it goes next is up to you, so keep it somewhere you trust.

Android backups

Memory Vault turns off Android's automatic cloud backup and its device-to-device transfer, so Android does not copy your vault to your Google account or to a new phone. The only backups are the files you export yourself.

Bug reports you choose to send

If something goes wrong, tap You, then Something's off. Memory Vault writes a report and opens your own email app with it addressed to me. You can read it first, and nothing is sent unless you press send yourself.

The report contains

  • the app version, Android version, phone model and WebView version
  • your screen size
  • how many memories, threads, dreams and photos you have
  • whether the passphrase lock is on
  • counts of slow frames and errors, with any quoted text removed
  • if you choose to include it, a "ghost picture" of the screen that shows only shapes, such as dots, outlines and grey bars, with no words

It never includes the words of your memories or dreams, or your photos. When you send it, I also receive your email address, as with any email.

I use these reports and your email address only to fix problems and reply to you. I do not share them with anyone, and I delete them once the issue is resolved or whenever you ask.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you remove it. Uninstalling Memory Vault, or going to Settings, Apps, Memory Vault, Storage and tapping Clear storage, deletes everything in the app, including your photos and the backup copy. Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them, so delete those there.

I hold none of your journal data. If you have sent me bug reports, email me and I will delete them.

Security

Android keeps the app's private storage separate from other apps, and the optional passphrase lock adds encryption on top. Locking your phone with a PIN, pattern, password or fingerprint helps protect it further. Memory Vault itself sends nothing over the internet, so your entries are never exposed in transit.

Children

Memory Vault is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If Memory Vault changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if it ever needs internet access, I will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy can go to whyjf at whyjf.hotmail@gmail.com.
