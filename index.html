Memory Vault Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026 · Developer whyjf

Memory Vault is a private journal for memories and dreams, made by whyjf. In short, everything you write stays on your phone, and I never see it.

What Memory Vault stores

Memory Vault saves the memories, dreams and settings you enter in the app's private storage on your device. That is the only place they are kept. The app has no internet access, so it cannot send your entries anywhere.

What I collect and share

Nothing. Memory Vault does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data or usage data. There is no account or sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting service. I have no server and no way to see what you write.

Permissions

Memory Vault does not request internet access. It does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Device backups

Memory Vault does not take part in Android's automatic backup to your Google account, so your entries are not copied off your phone.

Bug reports you choose to send

If something goes wrong, the "Something's off" option under You writes a report that you can email to me. The report is made on your phone and is only sent if you choose to send it, from your own email app. It never includes your memories or dreams. It may include basic technical details such as the app version, Android version and device model.

I use these reports, and the email address you send them from, only to fix problems and reply to you. I do not share them with anyone, and I delete them once the issue is resolved or whenever you ask.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your entries stay on your device until you delete them. You can delete an entry inside the app, or remove everything at once by uninstalling Memory Vault or by going to Settings, Apps, Memory Vault, Storage and tapping Clear storage.

Because I hold none of your journal data, there is nothing for me to delete on my side, apart from any bug report emails you have sent me.

Security

Your entries are kept in the app's private storage, which Android keeps separate from other apps. Locking your phone with a PIN, pattern, password or fingerprint is the best way to protect them. Memory Vault itself sends nothing over the internet, so your entries are never exposed in transit.

Children

Memory Vault is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If Memory Vault changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if it ever needs internet access, I will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy can go to whyjf at whyjf.hotmail@gmail.com.
